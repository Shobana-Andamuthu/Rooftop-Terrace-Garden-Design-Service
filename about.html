OUR HERITAGE • CRAFT & PASSION

From Concrete Rooftops to Verdant Sanctuaries

Founded in 2011 by landscape architect Julian Ross and structural engineer Maya Lin-Carter, TerraVerde was born out of a shared obsession: turning barren city rooftops into lush, biodiversity-rich private paradises.

Over fifteen years, our multidisciplinary team has engineered over 350 complex rooftop transformations across New York, London, Tokyo, and Stockholm, pioneering lightweight root matrices and IoT solar drip systems.

CORE PRINCIPLES

Our Design & Engineering Philosophy

Four non-negotiable architectural tenets guide every terrace commission we undertake.

Structural Integrity

Every planter, tree anchor, and pergola is calculated precisely against building load allowances and high-altitude wind shear forces.

Urban Biodiversity

We curate native nectar-rich flora, evergreen windbreaks, and drought-hardy perennials that restore vital ecological corridors in dense urban cores.

Smart Water Autonomy

Solar-driven sub-surface drip irrigation and closed-loop rainwater recycling prevent water waste while keeping plants consistently hydrated.

Sensory Harmony

From the aroma of Mediterranean lavender to the soft rustle of feather grasses, we design multisensory spaces for true psychological restorative calm.
