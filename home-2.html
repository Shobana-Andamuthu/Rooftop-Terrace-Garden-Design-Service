ARCHITECTURAL DELIVERY FRAMEWORK

Our 4-Stage Architectural Sky Transformation

From certified structural load calculations to off-site modular pre-assembly and precision botanical commissioning.

PHASE 01

Structural Load & Wind Shear Audit

Licensed structural engineers analyze dead/live load roof thresholds, membrane integrity, waterproofing barriers, and high-altitude wind uplift forces.

PHASE 02

3D Microclimate & Solar Simulation

Digital sunlight tracking and thermal vortex mapping determine custom shade pergola positioning, windbreak glass heights, and tailored plant species zoning.

PHASE 03

Modular Off-Site Prefabrication & Hoisting

Custom Corten troughs, lightweight fiber-clay planters, and sub-deck support pedestals are precision-built off-site for rapid crane assembly with zero rooftop mess.

PHASE 04

Horticultural Commissioning & Telemetry Sync

Acclimated mature specimens are planted with enriched aeration soil, living walls are plumbed, and automated solar drip sensors are calibrated.
